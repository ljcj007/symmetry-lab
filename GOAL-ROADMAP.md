## Remaining work beyond this release

- Audit every major course's prerequisites, terminology, worked examples, and next-step links.
- Add dedicated exercises where the reviewed crystal/Lie lesson has no corresponding task, rather than treating a same-track default as a match.
- Develop diagnostic entry questions and targeted remediation beyond the introductory bridge.
- Inventory model boundaries and exact/numeric computation support across the full tool catalogue.
- Extend cross-module material projects where the physical connection and inputs are explicit.
- Verify representative real-file import/export and scientific workflows for each major tool family.

This roadmap preserves the full objective. A single navigation release does not
establish that all existing scientific and teaching content has been audited.
