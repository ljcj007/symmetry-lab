\subsection*{Reusable contracts and structure-preserving certification}
The reusable object is a source-bound inference contract. A source supplies its
operator, carrier or metric, parameter domain and arithmetic premises. A spectral
receiver supplies complete ordered counts at common cuts, including complementary
directions. Closed-domain receipts are composed only after their source identities,
frames, remainder budgets and cover agree. Symmetry obstructions, positive spectral
isolation and topological assignments then select their additional premises rather
than following one mandatory chain ending in topology. In particular, transferring
a common interval by an operator bound does not by itself transfer a
symmetry-protected invariant.

The numerical ingredients have substantial precedents: plane-wave a posteriori
error control\cite{herbstNonscf,practicalPWBounds}, subspace bounds and certified
eigenspace dimensions\cite{sirkovicSubspace,certifiedMOR}, whole-zone gap
proofs\cite{hoangPhotonic}, verified matrix
decompositions\cite{rumpDecompositions}, and correlation-preserving
arithmetic\cite{affineArithmetic}. The present construction connects those tasks
to the identities of electronic states and to complete counts through changes of
cutoff carrier. Its source-specific proof mechanisms retain positive kinetic and
nonlocal-factor structure, or signed channels and shared momentum dependence.

Matched tests isolate their computational effect. In silicon box 000, preserving
the positive structure strengthens the auxiliary floor from approximately
$2.119$ to $2.759$~Ry with the centre, trial frame and original remainder budgets
fixed, completing a proof left unresolved by norm transport. In the signed-carbon
test, the same cuts and trial matrices pass both strict sign tests when projected
factors are assembled with their shared displacement before interval evaluation.
These observations establish a gain in sufficient proof strength for the tested
inputs. They do not establish a universal speed ranking or remove the need for
source-specific remainder and carrier proofs.
